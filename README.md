# 🎈 Pop Drop

**Pop Drop** is a sleek, modern physics-based arcade merge game built with HTML5 Canvas, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS. Inspired by classic drop-and-merge mechanics, players strategically drop various custom-themed pieces to combine matching items, trigger high-multiplier combos, and beat high scores before the board overflows!

---

## ✨ Features

* **Physics-Based Merge Mechanics:** Smooth collision detection and realistic physics using sub-stepping.
* **Responsive Touch & Mouse Controls:** Optimized for both mobile and desktop—simply tap or click anywhere on the screen to instantly track and drop pieces.
* **Dynamic Combo System:** Chain multiple merges together in a single turn to multiply your score and trigger screen shakes.
* **Custom Themes & Skins:** Switch between 8 unique item skin categories:
  * 🎈 Classic Balloons
  * 🧱 Tetris Bricks
  * 🏀 Neon Balls
  * ⭐ Star Orbs
  * 💖 Love Hearts
  * 💎 Crystal Gems
  * 😎 Smiley Faces
  * 🍉 Juicy Fruits
* **Web Audio Sound Effects:** Built-in procedural sound effects (pops, drops, beeps, and game-over alerts) generated dynamically with the Web Audio API.
* **High Score Tracking:** Local storage support to save and display your highest achievement.
* **Mobile Friendly:** Fully responsive, modern glassmorphism UI designed to fit any screen size or orientation without scrolling.

---

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Aim:** Drag, tap, or move your mouse horizontally across the screen to position your next dropping piece.
2. **Drop:** Release or tap to drop the piece into the container.
3. **Merge:** When two matching items touch each other, they merge into a larger, higher-tier item and award points!
4. **Survive:** Keep items below the danger line at the top. If a piece settles too high and crosses the line, the game is over.
